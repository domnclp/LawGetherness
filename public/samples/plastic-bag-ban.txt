Ordinance No. 2026-21: Banning Single-Use Plastic Bags

Section 1. All stores, market stalls, and food vendors are prohibited from giving out single-use plastic bags, including labo and sando bags.
Section 2. Customers must bring their own reusable bags or containers. Stores may sell paper bags for no more than ₱5 each.
Section 3. Violators shall be fined ₱500 for the first offense, ₱1,500 for the second offense, and suspension of the business permit for the third offense.
Section 4. The Municipal Environment Office and barangay officials shall conduct inspections.
Section 5. This ordinance takes effect 30 days after publication.
