Ordinance No. 2026-14: Regulating Tricycles on the National Highway

Section 1. Tricycles are prohibited from operating on the national highway within the municipality from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM daily.
Section 2. Tricycles may cross the national highway only at designated crossing points.
Section 3. Violators shall be fined ₱1,000 for the first offense, ₱2,000 for the second offense, and impoundment of the unit for the third offense.
Section 4. The Municipal Traffic Office and barangay tanods shall enforce this ordinance.
Section 5. This ordinance takes effect 15 days after publication.
