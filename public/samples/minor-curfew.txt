Ordinance No. 2026-09: Curfew for Minors

Section 1. Persons below 18 years old are prohibited from staying in streets, parks, and other public places from 10:00 PM to 4:00 AM without a parent or guardian.
Section 2. Minors found violating the curfew shall be brought to the barangay hall and released only to a parent or guardian.
Section 3. Parents or guardians shall be fined ₱500 for the first offense and ₱1,000 for succeeding offenses, or render community service.
Section 4. Barangay tanods and the Philippine National Police shall enforce this ordinance.
Section 5. This ordinance takes effect immediately upon publication.
